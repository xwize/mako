## Checks before close

This workspace runs checks (`pre_close_hook`) when you call `close_issue`. They run in your
worktree, on your branch, before anything is merged:

{pre_close_commands}
If one fails, the close is refused and nothing changes: the card stays In Progress and your
claim is kept. The error names the failing command and its log and shows the end of its output.
Fix the problem, commit, and call `close_issue` again. You can't skip the checks.

The checks can be slow. Run them yourself in your worktree before you close (after the
`git merge {base}`), so a close doesn't fail on something you could have caught. Commit
everything first: committing during the checks makes the close refuse, and you'd have to close
again.
